# Community Code of Conduct

Baixia Scroll welcomes people of every background and experience level. This
policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and spaces representing
the project. Participation does not require agreement on historical or artistic
interpretations; it does require respect for other people.

## Expected conduct

- Be considerate, patient and constructive. Critique the work, not the person.
- Respect different identities, cultures, abilities and levels of expertise.
- Explain evidence and uncertainty when discussing cultural or historical claims.
- Respect attribution, licenses, privacy and other people's boundaries.
- Accept feedback and stop unwelcome behavior when asked.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discriminatory slurs, sexualized attention, doxxing,
impersonation, sustained disruption and retaliation against a reporter are not
welcome. Do not publish another person's private information or private messages.

## Reporting

For incidents visible on GitHub, use GitHub's **Report abuse** control when
appropriate. To contact the project maintainer, open an issue titled
**Code of Conduct: request private contact**, without naming other people or
including private details. The maintainer will arrange a private channel before
requesting further information. Do not attach sensitive evidence to public issues.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews reports in context, keeps private reports confidential to
the extent practical, and gives affected participants an opportunity to respond.
Responses may include a correction request, warning, content removal, temporary
restriction or ban, depending on severity and recurrence. Severe threats or
harassment may result in immediate restriction. Retaliation is itself a violation.
Concerns about a decision may be raised through the same reporting process; a
maintainer involved in the incident should seek an uninvolved reviewer when one
is available. GitHub's own policies and enforcement also apply.
